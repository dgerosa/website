The name says it all: *spinformation*! Measuring spins in gravitational-wave events is a tricky business. There are two spins, so 6 numbers (both are vectors), but we actually mostly measure some combinations of these and the black-hole masses. These are the effective spins people talk about, and it’s actually unclear whether we measure *only* the effective spins or *mostly* the effective spins. That’s what we try to investigate in this paper, going beyond effective spins and chasing the *spinformation* in our GW catalog.

C. Plunkett, J. Heinzel, N. E. Wolfe, **D. Gerosa**, S. Vitale.\
[arXiv:2609.31879 [astro-ph.HE]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31879).
