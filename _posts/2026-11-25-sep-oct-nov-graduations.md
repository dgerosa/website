We've had three graduation sessions recently (September, October, November), with students from the group graduating in all of them. Here's a quick update (with congrats to all the new graduates, and thanks for working with me and my group!).

- **Sofia Dossena** got her MSc with an astro project on predicting the spin distributions of merging black holes (which is already published! [arXiv:2609.31879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31879)), working with me and Tristan Bruel. She's now a PhD student at SISSA in Trieste.
- **Lisa Merlo** got her MSc working with me, and a bit with Matteo "Bormio" Bonetti, on warped accretion disks. She's now a PhD student in my group.
- **Serena Caslini** got her MSc working with Nick Loutrel on timing neutron-star bursts. She's now a PhD student at GSSI in L’Aquila.
- **Giulia Foroni** got her MSc working with me and Vishal Baibhav (Columbia) on spins and eccentricity in binary black holes.
- **Ecaterina Tsuhuy** got her BSc (in AI, not Physics!) with a fun project on developing a machine-learning interpolator for gravitational-wave detectability.
- **Andrea La Canna** got his BSc working with Tristan Bruel on population-synthesis simulations.
- **Diego Noseda** got his BSc working with Alex Toubiana on galactic binaries in LISA.
- **Nicola Toselli** got his BSc working with Caroline Owen on the limits of the post-Newtonian expansion.

What a set! Next graduations are in February and March, I guess...
